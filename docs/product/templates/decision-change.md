## Decision request

Explain the recommendation, unknown impact and why changing an accepted decision needs confirmation under the [specification rule](../../engineering/rules/specifications.md). Recommend consulting the original owner/team and identify any missing attribution. Ask whether to retain, consult first or approve the named replacement. A record cannot authorize a message to someone else.
