Purpose

This policy sets how long Northstar Studio keeps operational records and who may approve an exception. It replaces v2 from August.

Retention periods

RecordKeep forThen
Support tickets24 monthsDelete
Application logs180 days 90 daysDelete
Incident reports5 yearsDelete Archive
Access reviews3 yearsArchive

Exceptions

An exception keeps a record longer than its period. Only the department lead and the Org Admin together may approve one, and each exception expires after 12 months unless renewed.

  • Legal hold overrides every period until it is lifted.
  • Customer deletion requests override "keep for" for personal data only.
  • Backups follow the same periods; restored data is re-deleted within 7 days.

Ownership

Engineering owns application logs. Operations owns support tickets and incident reports. Each owner checks once per quarter that deletion jobs ran and records the result in the access review.

What changed since v2

Application logs drop from 180 to 90 days. Incident reports are archived instead of deleted so post-mortems stay searchable.
