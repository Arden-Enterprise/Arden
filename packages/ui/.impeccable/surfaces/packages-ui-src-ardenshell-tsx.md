# Graph home — direction contract

Mode: Operate.

FORM seed key: `19682914` (`concept-seed --scope direction --mode operate`). The assigned calibrated-rail direction informed the one-line dividers, subdued state marks, and alignment discipline. Its literal rail topology would displace the user-requested, Obsidian-like graph home, so the user-pinned graph-first workspace remains the surface form. The user explicitly chose code-first construction and a sample network.

Purpose: Help a person orient in an organization's connected knowledge, inspect provenance and visibility, and move into a relevant item. The current implementation is a sample-only interface preview, not a live product feature.

Visual world: A calm, calibrated knowledge workspace. Warm-white paper-like working field, charcoal typography and key selections, mist rules, stone-grey secondary detail. Square geometry softened by 6–8 px corners. No bright accent, gradients, decorative metrics, or simulated official company claims. The graph itself supplies visual complexity; chrome remains quiet.

First viewport: At a desktop working size, show navigation, graph title and functional sample label, search/filter and zoom controls, a readable connected network, and a right Knowledge Pane with the selected sample item's summary, authority, provenance, and relationships. The graph must occupy the dominant share of the window.

Signature interaction: Selecting a node focuses its immediate neighborhood and updates the Knowledge Pane. Search and scope filters act on the same sample graph; keyboard focus and Escape restore are usable. No AI or content-editing behavior is faked.

Motion: A single restrained 160–220 ms emphasis transition for graph focus; the interface is otherwise steady. Respect reduced motion.

Adaptation: The three-column layout compresses to a two-column workspace, then a single column where the pane becomes a section below the graph. Keep the sample label and controls visible.

Content boundaries: All sample nodes, people, dates, relationships, and counts must be clearly presented as illustrative data. Product capability copy must distinguish implemented preview interactions from future server, review, and AI capabilities.

Success: Someone can recognize the graph-first product and locate an item, its source, and its status within seconds, without believing the preview contains their organization's data.

## Quality bar

- At 1440 × 900, the graph is the dominant working field; selected node, source, status, and relationships are legible without opening a modal.
- At 390 px, the graph's selected neighborhood remains readable and pannable, while a nearby selected-item summary and list provide direct access to all sample items.
- Muted nodes remain readable; emphasis is conveyed by mark/edge weight, not low-contrast text. Active text and secondary labels meet a 4.5:1 contrast target.
- Selection, search, scope filter, zoom, pan, Fit, and Escape reset work. Unimplemented destinations and AI are visibly marked as planned.
- All demonstration content stays labeled sample data. No actual organization access, AI answer, or external synchronization is implied.
