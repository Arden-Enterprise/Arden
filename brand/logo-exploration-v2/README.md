# Arden logo exploration, second pass

The first pass read too much like typography samples. This version pairs a drawn symbol with a custom vector wordmark, so every option is a complete logo and can also scale down to an app mark.

## Design brief

Arden is private organizational memory: knowledge is captured, related to current work, permissioned, reviewed, and then trusted. The visual identity should feel clear, assured, human, and enduring. It should work in warm white, mist, grey, and charcoal without relying on color effects.

## Palette

- Warm white: `#F4F3EF`
- Mist: `#D4D5D5`
- Stone grey: `#929698`
- Charcoal: `#242628`

All SVGs are path-based and use `currentColor`. They default to warm white; when embedded inline, set the parent CSS `color` to any palette value. The showcase switches between charcoal, grey, and light surfaces.

## Directions

1. **Open Index** — a solid custom A with an aperture and a small connection leaving its crossbar. Recommended first choice: specific to the name and the graph idea, while staying bold enough for a desktop icon.
2. **Folio** — paired page forms meet at a central seam. It speaks to collected organizational knowledge and works without a letter cue.
3. **Quiet Core** — an open orbit around a central index, representing private information that can connect outward under control.
4. **Source Route** — an asymmetric connected path with a clear central node, reflecting relationships between notes, tasks, and source material.
5. **Layered Archive** — three offset records converge into one current item, nodding to drafts, versions, and published knowledge.
6. **Canopy** — a compact branching structure with a stable trunk, connecting the Arden name's natural feel to growing organizational memory.

The `ARDEN` wordmark uses Bodoni Moda at a display optical size and medium weight, with custom tracking and optical placement. Its letters are saved as editable SVG outlines, so the logo renders consistently without the font installed. The supporting type direction is a restrained neo-grotesk (`Helvetica Neue` / Arial). Each symbol direction has a complete lockup and an individual SVG for app icons and other compact placements; the recommended Open Index wordmark is also available on its own. Bodoni Moda is distributed under the [SIL Open Font License](https://github.com/google/fonts/blob/main/ofl/bodonimoda/OFL.txt).

Open `index.html` to compare complete lockups and symbol-only versions against the grayscale palette. Choose a direction or identify elements to combine; this is still an exploration, so final optical kerning and small-size tuning can follow your selection.
